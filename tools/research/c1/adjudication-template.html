Label guide

A: works as-is; ordinary quantity adjustment is fine.

C: works only with a meaningful quantity, technique, or companion-ingredient change.

N: does not work for the dish or ingredient role, is too vague, or is the original ingredient again.

U: cannot be resolved from the available context or knowledge. Unknown is not negative; do not guess.

Choose the final A/C/N/U judgment using the provided context. Use U when unresolved; do not infer missing facts. Rationale is optional after choosing a label.
