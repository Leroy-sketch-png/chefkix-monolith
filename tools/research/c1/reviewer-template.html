Recipe review

The four answers
A - Works as-is

Same dish. Ordinary quantity adjustment is fine.

C - With changes

Needs a meaningful quantity, technique, or companion-ingredient change.

N - Does not work

Breaks the dish or ingredient's role, is too vague, or is the original ingredient again.

U - Cannot judge

Not enough context or knowledge. Unknown is not a negative.

Judge every suggestion independently in its recipe, not just your favourite. Work separately from the other reviewer; do not use AI to decide. No cooking or tasting is required. These are culinary judgments, not allergy or safety approvals.
