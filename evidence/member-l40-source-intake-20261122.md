# L40 private source intake request for the source and study owners

Use this request with the [adopted L40 design](lead-l40-vietnamese-pilot-design-freeze-20261106.md). Return restricted storage paths and receipt hashes over the approved private channel. The Member can use the replies to validate the case frame, draw the sample, and prepare reviewer payloads.

## Source custodian

Provide the existing Vietnamese recipe source inventory with its source owner, version/date, restricted locator, and a per-record rights reference. For each planned operation, identify the permission evidence or mark it unresolved: private storage, adaptation, frozen GISMo processing, blinded reviewer access, and aggregate reporting. Include any access, retention, attribution, or reporting conditions that apply. A ChefKix database entry alone gives neither a source version nor a per-case permission record.

For each candidate case, provide the exact recipe and target-slot identities, cluster identity, original Vietnamese context, evidenced region, primary dish family, and a checked secondary English gloss. For declared-allergen cases, provide the declared group and the exact ingredient form with its supporting source relation and version. Identify the custodians of the frozen GISMo checkpoint, vocabulary/catalog, and exact case/target mapping.

## Study owner

Identify the two source contributors, including the native cook, the qualified bilingual gloss reviewer, the two qualified independent Vietnamese-speaking cooks, and the separate adjudicator. Return the existing study authorization or restricted reference needed for role assignment, private reviewer delivery and return handling. Keep confidential institutional records in restricted storage.

## Member finish condition

Member records reason counts for exclusions, verifies both cohorts against their fixed quotas, and freezes the eligible frame before outputs. Missing evidence excludes only its affected record or cohort under the existing shortfall rule. Member then builds the scoped blinded R1/R2 payloads, verifies the restricted mapping and exact slate, and records only permitted aggregates and hashes in the repository. Reviewer delivery and study acceptance require the governing assignments and permissions.
