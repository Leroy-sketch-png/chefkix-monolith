# L42 bounded adjudication: S43/S46/S47/S48 against the proposed candidate-validity audit

Date: 2026-11-05. This is a bounded Lead adjudication of four existing source screens, not closure of L42 or a global novelty finding.

## Question and comparison unit

The narrow question is whether the exact proposed IRON CHEF human-audit design has already been demonstrated by the two closest cited sources: for one fixed recipe and target ingredient, place outputs from multiple methods plus the recorded gold in a common pool; conceal method and rank; label each candidate in recipe context with culinary-function A/C/N/U; then compare exact-gold Hit with the presence of any independently adjudicated acceptable candidate, including on unseen-identity strata. This is a proposed estimand/design, not an operation fully frozen by Protocol v1: §2 freezes the conditional rescue estimand and A/C/N/U handling, while the audit's pool construction and unresolved PR01/PR03 decisions remain open in the strategy.

## Lead conclusion and falsifier

The defensible thesis wording today is a **research question**, not a novelty claim: *Does exact-match retrieval against a single recorded substitute undercount independently judged, context-valid alternatives within a recipe-context candidate set, and does that gap differ across the frozen identity strata?* The proposed audit could test that question if its protocol decisions are resolved and the appropriate evidence is collected. Do not say the method is new, the answer is yes, or that it establishes safety or human usefulness.
