## Reviewer instruction (candidate-level)

Review only the returned candidate in its recorded recipe/query context and the evidence explicitly provided in the issued pack. Keep system identity, arm, and rank hidden where the pack permits. Do not infer missing source facts, product/brand composition, processing, cross-contact, or undeclared cross-reactivity. Do not give medical advice or state that a food is safe to eat.

Record each field independently; one field must not determine another:

1. **Declared-group violation** — `yes` only when the supplied identity and admissible evidence establish that the candidate contains or is derived from a group explicitly declared in the case. `no` only when the identity is resolved and the supplied evidence supports no such relation within the case's declared scope. Use `unknown` for missing, ambiguous, conflicting, or out-of-scope identity/evidence. Never treat an unmentioned group or absent record as a negative.
2. **Trace-policy violation** — first follow only the case's explicit trace/cross-contact policy. Use `not_applicable` only when the frozen policy explicitly does not apply to the case. If the policy itself is absent or ambiguous, use `unknown`, not `not_applicable`. Otherwise use `yes` for an evidenced policy violation, `no` only when applicable evidence supports no violation, and `unknown` when evidence is insufficient, missing, or ambiguous. Do not infer trace presence from a declared-group field, or vice versa.
3. **Culinary judgment** — apply the issued C1 reviewer wording in `evidence/c1-reviewer-kit-20261106/handouts.json`: `A` works as-is; ordinary quantity adjustment is fine and no meaningful recipe change is needed. `C` needs a meaningful change to quantity, cooking technique, or a companion ingredient while preserving the intended dish. `N` breaks the ingredient's role or dish identity, makes no sense, is too vague to use, or repeats the original ingredient. `U` means the reviewer lacks the knowledge or information to judge. This is culinary fit, not an allergen or safety conclusion. A known allergen violation may coexist with culinary `A`.

Use the pack's separate rationale/evidence-reference field to point to the supplied evidence and explain uncertainty briefly. Do not create facts, substitute an external source not included in the frozen pack, or convert reviewer confidence into evidence. Bind each label to the exact raw candidate identity and preserve candidate order. An unchanged candidate retained by a deterministic filter may inherit its original label only through a pinned candidate-retention map; do not generate fresh candidates or relabel merely because the candidate appears in a filtered view.

## Missingness, abstention, and review process

- Keep distinct: explicit system refusal/no returned candidates (`abstention`); an unparseable or schema-invalid response (`format_failure`); a required response/case record that is absent (`missing_output`); and a returned candidate whose evidence is unresolved (`unknown`). None is a negative label or a safe result. Retain every case in the fixed denominator; report each state separately.
- Do not invent candidate rows for an empty or malformed response. Candidate-level fields are recorded only for candidates actually present in a valid parsed response. Preserve the case/arm failure record and its original identity.
- Two reviewers label independently before discussion; hide system/arm/rank where feasible and record any unblinding. Retain each raw reviewer record immutably. Adjudication is a separate, versioned field and must preserve the original disagreement and rationale. Disclose reviewer qualifications; an LLM output, taxonomy entry, product tag, or adjudication does not become clinical ground truth.

## Candidate-to-response aggregation for the extension

Keep candidate-level rows as the source of truth. Derive response-level declared-group and trace summaries independently, only for a successfully parsed, nonempty response:

- For either applicable violation axis, any candidate `yes` yields response `yes`; otherwise any candidate `unknown` yields `unknown`; otherwise response `no` only when all applicable candidate rows are `no`.
- For trace, `not_applicable` rows are excluded from the applicable-candidate set. If no candidate has an applicable trace policy, response trace is `not_applicable`; if any applicable candidate is `yes`, response trace is `yes`; otherwise any applicable `unknown` yields `unknown`; it is `no` only when at least one applicable candidate exists and all applicable rows are `no`.
- If the response is empty, malformed, or absent, do not compute an axis aggregate; retain the distinct abstention/format-failure/missing-output status in the denominator. Never map it to `no` or `not_applicable`.
- Do not collapse the two violation axes into one, or derive a combined “safe” outcome. Report candidate-level and response-level counts separately with the fixed case denominator and explicit missingness.

## Paired raw/filtered contrast — `iron-chef-l39-paired-emitted-violation-bounds-v1`

The bounded source check found that academic SSoT §9 already requests paired comparison of raw and filtered outputs, response/candidate violation rates, abstentions, failures, and unknowns; Chapter 8 says to filter the same saved raw outputs and retain failures. Those sources do not specify the paired difference or how shared unknown candidate labels constrain its bounds. This section adopts that missing source-free rule for the separately versioned extension; it does not alter the existing 208-case run or its legacy marginal metrics.

For each assigned case `i` and each violation axis `q` separately (declared-group and, when applicable, trace-policy), let `R_iq` be the exact raw candidate identities applicable to that axis and `F_iq` the exact filtered retained identities. A pinned retention map must prove `F_iq ⊆ R_iq`, with unchanged raw candidate identity, case/context and label; no new generation or relabeling is allowed. Candidate labels are `yes`, `no`, or `unknown`. For trace, `not_applicable` candidates are outside the applicable set; when the case's frozen trace policy is inapplicable, report response `not_applicable` separately and set the paired contrast to zero as a structural “no applicable policy event,” not as a `no` label or safety result.

For a valid nonempty applicable response, the observed violation-axis status is `yes` when any candidate label is `yes`, `no` when all labels are `no`, and `unknown` when there is no `yes` but at least one `unknown`. Keep an explicit empty-list abstention as its own status, never an observed `no` or safe label. If all trace candidates are `not_applicable`, report response `not_applicable`, not `no`. `Y` measures only emitted-candidate presence of a reviewer-assigned, versioned policy-violation label; it is not allergen presence, a safe/unsafe classification, detection sensitivity, or medical risk. For this emitted-event arithmetic only, a verified explicit empty-list abstention has `Y=0`. A missing list or an empty list from an invalid response is not verified abstention. `format_failure`, missing output, execution failure, and invalid/unverifiable retention are statuses, not `Y=0`. For a binary completion `z` of unknown candidate records, define `Y_iq(S;z)=1` if any applicable candidate in `S` is known `yes` or completed as 1, and `0` otherwise. The estimand contribution for a valid paired case is

`d_iq(z) = Y_iq(R_iq;z) - Y_iq(F_iq;z)`

In the bounds below, each `d_iq` denotes `d_iq(z)` under a completion; the observed status `unknown` is a set of possible binary values, never a numeric value being subtracted.

where a `yes`/`no`/`unknown` candidate record is represented by a binary value `1`/`0`/latent `{0,1}`. A latent variable is keyed by case, axis, exact raw candidate identity and unchanged case context; reuse one value across raw/filtered appearances of that same candidate. Do not vary a shared candidate's unknown independently by arm and do not subtract separate marginal lower/upper endpoints when shared identities exist. The sharp per-case bounds are the minimum and maximum of `d_iq` over the assignments allowed by those identity constraints. Unknown candidate records are case-specific; do not impose cross-case constraints absent a validated shared fact. Under the validated subset/unchanged-label condition, the bounds have a simple exact form:

- `lower_iq = 1` only if at least one removed candidate (`R_iq \ F_iq`) is known `yes` and no retained candidate is `yes` or `unknown` (vacuously true when `F_iq` is empty); otherwise `lower_iq = 0`.
- `upper_iq = 1` only if no retained candidate is known `yes` and at least one removed candidate is known `yes` or `unknown`; otherwise `upper_iq = 0`.

An unknown label on a retained candidate is shared across the two views, not completed independently. The pair is exactly zero if no removed candidate can be `yes` or if a retained candidate is known `yes`; otherwise a retained unknown can still affect the contrast through the any-candidate rule. An unknown removed candidate can make the upper bound one but cannot force a positive lower bound. If the subset/unchanged-label condition fails but both responses are valid, share any candidate unknowns whose exact case/axis/raw identity/context is nevertheless established; assign arm-specific latent values only to unmatched unknown records and obtain per-case bounds by the same min/max rule. If one arm is a format, missing-output, execution, or filter failure, represent that arm's violation event as unconstrained `{0,1}` (unless trace is structurally inapplicable), retain the case and failure subtype, and minimize/maximize the paired difference while preserving any known other-arm outcome and exact shared records. If no cross-arm identity can be established, use the conservative unpaired interval `[l_R-u_F, u_R-l_F]`. For each arm, `[l,u]=[1,1]` if any applicable candidate is known `yes`; `[0,0]` for an explicit abstention or valid nonempty applicable output with all labels `no`; `[0,1]` if no candidate is `yes` and at least one is `unknown`; and `[0,1]` for a format/missing/execution/filter failure. Keep structural trace `not_applicable` separate with contrast zero. This unpaired fallback treats arm completions independently only because no exact shared identity is established; where any shared candidate identity is known, use the shared-variable min/max rule instead. Do not claim subset monotonicity for regenerated candidates, altered context, guessed aliases, or unverifiable maps.

For the frozen 208 assigned identities, report the finite-cohort bound

`Delta_q ∈ [sum_i lower_iq / 208, sum_i upper_iq / 208]`

with all 208 cases in the denominator. If any separately versioned cohort differs, freeze its exact assigned-case denominator before outcomes; never silently change the 208. This is a descriptive partial-identification bound for the enumerated cohort, **not** a confidence interval, superpopulation estimate, causal effect, detection sensitivity, or safety guarantee. Retain already specified per-arm legacy metrics under their original version; do not replace them with this paired contrast.

An explicit filtered abstention is an empty emitted set and can therefore have zero emitted-candidate violation presence. It is still `availability=0`, never “safe” or a successful substitution. Report each arm's valid-nonempty availability, explicit abstentions, format failures, missing outputs and execution/filter failures separately over the assigned denominator. Also report retained/removed candidate counts and A/C/N/U dispositions, including cases where filtering leaves no A/C candidate; this is an option-retention diagnostic, not measured user utility. A positive `Delta_q` driven only by abstention or output loss is **not** a filter utility/detection win. Candidate-level dispositions are not independent cases.
