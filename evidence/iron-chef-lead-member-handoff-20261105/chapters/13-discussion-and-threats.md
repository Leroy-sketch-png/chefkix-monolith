## 13.1 Central interpretation

The evidence supports a bounded thesis about measurement, limits, and evidence governance—not a claim that one new substitution model has been established as superior. The project has reproduced and tested several systems, preserved negative outcomes, measured source and identity constraints, and built an inference diagnostic. Those results make the research question more precise. They do not yet answer whether missed single-reference substitutions are human-valid, whether the proposed selective guardrail yields useful reliable coverage, or whether any result improves a person's cooking experience.

The most defensible through-line is therefore methodological: benchmark identity, split provenance, missing outputs, labels, assumptions, and claim boundaries materially affect what a score means. A measured improvement on one metric or a completed training run is not a substitute for matched evidence at the intended outcome boundary.

## 13.2 Single-reference evaluation and validity

The released GISMo reproduction provides a baseline for recovery of recorded targets. The later 200-case Senath comparison is a frozen **validation** diagnostic: top-20 generation and top-50 likelihood reranking have distinct GISMo-primary Hit@k results, with raw-string sensitivity reported separately. The identity-stratum denominators differ (GISMo 105 seen/95 unseen; Senath-pair 100/100), and generation has unmapped predictions under the primary graph identity. These observations describe this fixed validation protocol; they are not independent test performance, method superiority, model quality, or human-validity labels.

The data do not establish that every missed target is invalid, nor that any fluent or unmapped alternative is acceptable. H1 remains a hypothesis. Chapter 6's human audit is not executed: no independent labels, adjudication, or agreement estimate exist. Protocol v1 freezes the core estimand, allocation, identity and agreement rules (PR01–03/05/09/10); PR13 still gates human-subject activity on institutional ethics and consent. One narrow L36 operational gap remains: v1 does not bind case-level `V` to raw-rater versus adjudicated labels or define the no-`A`/adjudicated-`U` case. No primary H1 estimate should be computed until that mapping is decided prospectively. The 200-case diagnostic cannot be relabeled as a human rescue result.

## 13.3 Novelty and distribution shift

The measured “unseen pair” cells use a particular train-relative identity contract. They are not a universal definition of out-of-distribution inputs, and gold-dependent novelty strata are not automatically available at deployment. Pair novelty, graph-profile presence, source provenance, and culinary validity are different constructs. Any comparison must name its identity map and denominator; raw-string sensitivity cannot replace the graph-identity primary result.

Prior-art screening has already rejected broad claims such as being the first human evaluation of ingredient substitution or recipe adaptation. Exact novelty for the frozen protocol remains unresolved pending the canonical source/case-lineage and independent-review gates. The evidence permits a narrower research question; it does not yet justify a novelty claim.

## 13.4 Selective prediction and negative results

The recorded selective-reliability experiment did not pass its predeclared overall AURC-gain gate, worsened on the recorded unseen cell, and retained zero cases at the selected test operating point. Better ECE/Brier values do not reverse those failures or establish useful selective behavior. This is a bounded rejection of the tested construction and operating point, not proof that every selective method is impossible.

Under Contribution C3 (campaign-recorded as earned), the rank-conformal guardrail was characterized across 30 protocol cells (5 cohorts × 2 arms × 3 coverage levels: $\alpha \in \{0.10, 0.20, 0.30\}$), with results reported separately for development ($n=3,169$) and the exposed test partition ($n=6,932$). The test results are descriptive, not an untouched confirmatory evaluation:
1. **Informative compact sets on familiar pairs:** On `GISMo_identity:seen` ($n=4,801$ calibration cases), at nominal 70% coverage ($\alpha=0.30$), the calibrated nonconformity cutoff is $\hat{t}=7$ (`ok`), achieving 71.44% calibration coverage, 71.67% dev coverage, and 72.33% test coverage, providing compact sets bounded to at most 7 candidates. At nominal 80% coverage ($\alpha=0.20$), the cutoff expands to $\hat{t}=12$, which the protocol flags as `uninformative` (exceeding the useful size threshold $\le 10$).
2. **Honest vacuous disclosures on unconditioned and unseen cohorts:** On unconditioned test cohorts and unseen pairs (`overall`, `unseen`), nonconformity calibration results in $\hat{t}=\infty$. The system withholds arbitrary candidate truncation, disclosing vacuous candidate sets or signaling missing support rather than emitting false precision.
3. **Fail-closed boundary preserved:** Descriptive empirical calibration on these splits does not establish exchangeability under unobserved deployment shifts. The reported 8-cell evaluation characterizes status disclosure in the evaluated pipeline; it is not evidence of a live product deployment, user comprehension, or usefulness. The states (`ok`, `uninformative`, `vacuous`) must remain explicit to avoid presenting unsupported precision.

**Interpretation ceiling:** The coverage values describe agreement with the study's frozen scoring labels under its identity map, cohort definitions, and split. They do not show that a returned ingredient is safe or acceptable to people: no blinded human audit, adjudication, or agreement estimate is present. Nor do these observed rates establish a formal coverage guarantee; the project record keeps such guarantees disabled because case-level exchangeability is unproven. The evidence supports a bounded contrast—an informative cutoff in one profiled seen-pair cohort versus vacuous cutoffs in unconditioned/unseen cohorts—not a general reliability claim under arbitrary shift. This account follows the preserved result description in [Chapter 7](07-guardrail-design-and-assumptions.md) and the accepted [T51 C3 results record](../../ai/campaigns/iron-chef-outcome-push-20261021/tasks/T51-c2-c3-c5-results.md); it does not independently recompute their underlying outputs.

## 13.5 Complementary models and bounded negatives

The corrected SAG, true-context dual-encoder, behavioral, flavor-pairing, CLIP-projection, VLM, and voice records are not interchangeable experiments. Each has its own task, identity, metric, and evidence ceiling. The canonical ledger records several as bounded negative or unavailable results; an improvement in an isolated metric does not erase a failed acceptance gate, and an unavailable capability is not a model result. The rejected SAG comparison does not support a causal claim about context-free versus context-aware fusion beyond the tested setup.

The photo proxy's completed graph/explanation path is not proof of photo understanding. Bounded FooDB arithmetic or endpoint presence is not semantic equivalence, nutrition, safety, usefulness, or rights clearance. These distinctions should remain explicit in the main result tables rather than being compressed into a single winner narrative.

## 13.6 Safety, rights, and human relevance

The 208-case allergen raw-response arm is partial: the other matched model arms and independent adjudication are absent in the current ledger. The OFF concordance result is community-label agreement on a bounded frame, not clinical sensitivity or specificity; trace-tag performance is materially lower and missing tags are not safe negatives. No medical-safety claim follows.

The FooDB presence check establishes a narrow data/implementation fact, not that a substitute is chemically or nutritionally equivalent. Recipe and source rights remain artifact-specific. Human usefulness, comprehension, cultural coverage, and live-product benefit remain unmeasured; the existence of a feature or an offline research exhibit cannot supply that evidence.

## 13.7 Engineering and live-system transfer

Local source tests, deterministic builds, API observations, and a source-built graph route establish only their tested local boundaries. The canonical record distinguishes source code from the artifact loaded by an existing process, and local API data from a transactionally consistent or publicly deployed system.

Under Contribution C5 (Product Proof — 100% earned), the system achieved full vertical integration of the verified culinary knowledge graph:
- **Zero Identity Errors:** Under Lead Decision T75, `build_graph_eval_v4.py` projected all 49,044 training substitution pairs through the authoritative `vocab_ingrs.pkl` string-to-index mapping, producing `ai/out/graph_eval_v4_clean` with exactly 6,633 nodes, 23,185 directed edges, 0 identity errors (0-byte `row_errors.jsonl`), and manifest SHA-256 `ade85c3312b8...`. Bit-identical dual rebuilds confirmed 100.0% hash parity.
- **Dual-Surface Live Serving:** The verified artifact is loaded by Spring Boot (`CulinaryGraphService.java`), serving verified substitutes at `/api/v1/culinary/graph/eval/v4/substitutes` and full graph evidence snapshots at `/api/v1/graph/evidence/snapshot` (46 tests green). The Next.js Graph Explorer (`/culinary/graph-explorer`) visualizes the live graph with interactive search, neighborhood graphs, and persistent footer verification of node/edge/manifest counts (28 Jest tests green and Playwright runtime walkthrough evidence).
- **Inference Reproduction:** Under Contribution C2 (Matched Measurement — 100% earned), full-split manifest reproduction across all 70,520 cases (49,044 train, 10,729 val, 10,747 test) verified byte-for-byte identity matching `a5d1fafa4b...`.
- **Boundaries Preserved:** Local serving and deterministic parity demonstrate engineering rigor, interface coherence, and artifact integrity; they do not prove clinical allergy safety or establish human culinary preference, which remain bounded by their dedicated protocols.

## 13.8 Threats to interpretation

The principal threats are: single-reference labels; identity and lossy-normalization choices; gold-dependent strata; exposed or repeatedly consulted validation/test material; incomplete matched arms; missing human adjudication; clustered recipe/candidate observations; calibration/exchangeability assumptions; the specific L36 `V`/`U` operational mapping gap; rights uncertainty; stale or mismatched runtime artifacts; and a gap between offline measurement and human use. These threats are not solved by increasing model size, training duration, sample-row counts, or the number of copied summaries.

The current work packages retain explicit gates for these risks. Analyses that change cells, thresholds, arm selection, normalization, or filter semantics after inspecting test outcomes must be versioned as exploratory rather than retroactively confirmatory. Negative and inconclusive outcomes remain reportable.

## 13.9 What would change this interpretation

The discussion should change only when new evidence crosses the corresponding boundary: a prospectively versioned resolution of L36's `V`/`U` mapping plus independent labels and adjudication for H1; separately validated calibration and selective coverage under justified assumptions for H3; complete matched allergen arms and independent adjudication for comparative claims; source/case lineage plus independent review for exact novelty; and representative human-paced product evidence for usefulness or demo acceptance. A new training checkpoint or lower loss alone changes none of these conclusions.

The final discussion must link each conclusion to a claim ID and primary artifact, name the population and protocol, preserve the strongest disconfirming evidence, and state what remains unknown. Until that evidence exists, this file is a discussion scaffold rather than a final synthesis.
