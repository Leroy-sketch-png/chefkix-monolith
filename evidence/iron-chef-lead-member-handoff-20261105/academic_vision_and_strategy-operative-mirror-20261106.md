## 2. Vision, thesis and hypotheses

**Question:** when do ingredient-substitution systems fail, when does a single recorded answer misrepresent validity, and can a system expose its uncertainty without overstating its evidence?

**Thesis as a research proposition:** aggregate single-answer scores may under-credit valid alternatives and conceal failures on unseen pairs, sparse evidence and under-represented cuisines. IRON CHEF builds an instrument to measure those effects and tests an allergen-aware, set-valued abstention guardrail over frozen scorers. It reports every cell, including insufficient-data, uninformative and vacuous outcomes. Whether filtering reduces measured violations and whether explanations help people are separate empirical questions—not presumed safety or usefulness.

| ID | Hypothesis | Measurement / proposed claim rule |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Some single-gold misses are culinary-valid, especially unseen pairs | Frozen Protocol v1: primary GISMo arm, unseen GISMo-identity stratum, strict valid@5; conditional rescue `P(V=1 | H=0)` with support/refutation at the 0.15 stratified-design 95% bound. Joint rescue is secondary. The eligible-miss denominator is weighted from the two unseen strata; it is not presumed to be 100 or 150. Before scoring, resolve the v1 omission of raw-versus-adjudicated `V` and adjudicated-`U` handling without editing v1. |
| H2 | Aggregates hide pair-novelty/evidence-coverage drops, with cuisine as pilot | Per-cell Hit@k, valid@k and intervals; pooled table never stands alone. |
| H3 | Some per-cell sets can be informative under explicit assumptions | Cost-of-trust/status table; proposed ≤10 suggestions at α=.2. PR04–PR08 gate any guarantee. |
| H4 | Bounded compound records improve human understanding | Ethics-governed usefulness and comprehension study, N≥20; not inferred from arithmetic factuality. |
| H5 | Complementary ranker/LLM arms could improve set size at equal coverage | Exploratory only after matched arms exist; no test-selected hybrid promoted as confirmatory. |

Outcome interpretation: substantial rescue shifts the emphasis toward benchmark validity; little rescue supports the bounded collapse finding; intervals crossing the decision threshold are inconclusive. Informative sets support a bounded guardrail result; vacuous sets document a limit of the tested construction/budget, not proof that every method is impossible. Poor rater agreement is a measurement limitation, not permission to invent consensus. All outcomes are reportable; committee approval, publication and commercial success are not guaranteed.
