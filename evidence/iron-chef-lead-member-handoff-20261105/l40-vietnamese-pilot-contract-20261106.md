## 2. Research question and permitted claim

**Proposed question:** On a deliberately varied, rights-cleared set of Vietnamese recipe-conditioned replacement requests, what do two independent native-speaking cooks record about the culinary acceptability of the frozen top-five candidate slate, and—on a separately counted set with a declared dietary constraint and source-backed non-obvious allergen context—what violation, unknown, withholding, and abstention states does the frozen system produce?

This is a bounded instrument/coverage diagnostic, not a representative survey of Vietnamese cuisine, causal cuisine comparison, clinical allergen-risk study, product-utility result, or formal conformal guarantee. Report the two cohorts separately. The contract does not select a model arm or decide L36's unresolved primary `V`/adjudicated-`U` mapping. Until that mapping and candidate arm are prospectively frozen, preserve candidate-level judgments and label any case-level `valid@5` summary as unavailable or explicitly exploratory; never recode `U` as negative.

Candidate rows and multiple raters are nested observations, not independent cases. Prefer at most one selected target per source-recipe cluster. If a frozen cohort must contain related variants, retain their common `recipe_cluster_id`, disclose the dependency, and do not count them as independent recipes.

An eligible record must have a usable Vietnamese title, ingredient list, target slot, and enough preparation/context to judge the target's culinary role; context insufficiency is a recorded status, not an inferred recipe step. A 40-case constraint record additionally needs an explicitly stated constraint and source-backed evidence for the intended non-obvious allergen-source context. Do not invent missing ingredients, preparation, regional origin, target identity, or allergen presence. Public recipes with unchecked or incompatible terms are ineligible. A first-party/authored source is usable only with a recorded rights grant covering the actual private storage, adaptation, model processing, reviewer access, and planned aggregate publication operations.

## 4. Identity, spelling, and food form

Preserve Vietnamese text in UTF-8 with all diacritics and original spacing/punctuation in the raw field. Lossy diacritic stripping, transliteration, English translation, fuzzy matching, or alias expansion may not define primary identity. A reversible Unicode NFC comparison field may be used only as a mechanical representation aid; it must preserve the raw bytes/string and must not collapse distinct terms. Any alias or cross-source identity requires a versioned native-reviewed mapping with source term, accepted identity, reviewer, rationale, and ambiguity state.

Ingredient concept and edible form are separate: preserve the named ingredient plus relevant plant/animal part, processed/fermented/dried/fresh state, cut or preparation, and role in the dish when known. Do not equate forms from a name alone. In the allergen cohort, form-sensitive evidence must bind the exact ingredient form to an authoritative/source-backed relation; missing relation means `unknown`, not a negative. The §9 examples (fish sauce, shrimp/krill pastes, peanut, sesame, soy/wheat condiments) are proposed coverage leads, not a complete or medically validated taxonomy.

## 5. Sampling and cohort coverage — prospective proposal

1. Build the eligible source frame and rights/identity/duplicate audit before model output, candidate labels, or results are viewed. Keep its private source manifest, case IDs, exclusions, duplicate/template clusters, and reason counts local.
2. Target `N≈100`: 60 culinary-validity and 40 hidden-allergen-constraint cases, each with its own fixed denominator and report. Proposed region allocation is 20/20/20 for the 60-case cohort and 14/13/13 for the 40-case cohort (largest-remainder split in the fixed North/Central/South order). This allocation is prospective and needs a design freeze before any draw.
3. Within each cohort, use the seven existing §9 dish families as primary coverage categories; target at least five cases per family in the 60-case cohort and three in the 40-case cohort where eligible source records exist. Sample within feasible region × family cells by deterministic round-robin toward the least-filled eligible cells, breaking ties with a precommitted seed and stable private case ID. Do not fabricate a cell or silently transfer a shortfall; publish only aggregate cell counts and record unfilled targets.
4. For the constraint cohort, annotate applicable §9 source-context categories separately. Seek at least five cases per represented category where the eligible source frame permits, but permit overlap and report each category's unique-case `n`; category counts do not sum to an independent sample size. Missing or ambiguous source evidence remains unknown and cannot be replaced by a convenient negative.
5. Check exact duplicates, near-duplicate templates, shared author/source lineage, and repeated target slots before freezing. Prefer one case per source-recipe cluster. If the target cannot be reached after rights/eligibility review, stop at the attained `N`, report the shortfall, and do not call the planned 100-case pilot complete.

The sample is purposive maximum-variation coverage, not a probability sample. Regional/dish quotas support coverage disclosure only; they do not establish population representation or a cuisine effect. Final quotas, seed, eligible-frame rule, cluster treatment, and arm slate are explicit pre-output freeze fields; they are not decided by looking at model or rater outcomes.

## 6. Language, candidate slate, split, and blinding

Vietnamese is the primary input and review language. Proposed default is an English gloss for every case, authored/checked by a qualified bilingual human, stored as a paired secondary field. The exact §10 alternative—a preselected random half—remains available if capacity requires it, but its seed and selection must be frozen before outputs. The English gloss is not model input or a replacement label in the primary Vietnamese assessment. A translation/back-translation handbook follows Chapter 9; translation discrepancies are preserved for review rather than silently normalized.

There is no training split: the whole pilot is evaluation-only and cannot tune models, prompts, candidate policy, identity mappings, thresholds, or quotas. The 60/40 cohort label is an analysis partition, not a random train/test split. Select the model arm(s), candidate-source hashes, and top-five construction before candidate generation. Reuse Chapter 4's slate contract: top five from each included arm plus a recorded gold only when the source actually provides one; deduplicate under the already-frozen identity rule while retaining raw forms and all arm/rank origins; deterministically shuffle; keep the mapping key sealed from reviewers and analysts preparing the blind pack.

Two qualified native-speaking cooks independently label every candidate under Chapter 4's rubric. Proposed independence rule: neither reviewer authored or selected the recipe, and both are blind to arm, rank, gold, and source-versus-model origin. Reviewer competence, native-language criterion, and cooking-experience evidence are recorded minimally and privately. Raw labels are immutable; adjudication is a distinct, versioned record. Model payloads contain only the frozen task inputs and, for constraint cases, the declared constraint—not reviewer labels, adjudication, hidden-source evidence, or the sealed mapping. Whether authors may participate as raters is not assumed; the proposed design keeps them separate.

## 7. Denominators, failures, and reporting

Freeze the eligible/enrolled case list before candidate generation. Preserve the flow from eligible → selected → frozen → attempted → labeled → analyzed, by cohort and aggregate region/family. Each frozen case receives either outputs or an explicit method/parse failure. Every scheduled candidate receives a label or explicit missing/withdrawn/`U` status; never delete a difficult case or treat `U`, missing profile, unresolved identity, or failed run as a negative or safe result.

Report at minimum, separately for each cohort and observed region/family/category: target `N`, frozen `N`, available `n`, missing/withdrawn/failure counts, candidate-level denominators, adjudication availability, and the fixed missingness/failure reasons. For culinary validity, report candidate-level A/C/N/U and a clearly defined case-level strict-A@5 summary only after the V/U rule and candidate slate are frozen; report A+C sensitivity separately. For constraints, report `yes/no/unknown/not_applicable`, source-evidence coverage, candidates removed/withheld, response-level violation/unknown/abstention, and empty-pool abstention separately. Human acceptability, source-backed allergen identity, and system behavior are different outcomes.

Use the existing Chapter 4/Protocol v1 agreement convention (Tier-1 kappa ≥0.5; claim “reliable agreement” only when the strict-kappa case-cluster-bootstrap 95% lower bound ≥0.6); report interval unavailable if the required valid bootstrap draws are insufficient. Report intervals only with their stated sampling assumptions and exact observed denominator. The purposive pilot does not earn a population or conformal guarantee. No pooled 60+40 headline, safety certification, clinical inference, causal cuisine comparison, or “native-valid” generalization is permitted.

Consent withdrawal takes precedence over denominator retention: delete a person's material when the approved consent/ethics procedure requires it; retain only a non-identifying flow count if that is allowed. Otherwise retain explicit withdrawn/missing status without imputing an outcome. The manifest and analysis must state which rule applied.
